Description

Upgrade your food drying process with our premium Food-Grade Silicone Mesh Sheets. If you are tired of fruits, vegetables, or jerky sticking to your metal dehydrator trays, these mesh mats are the ultimate solution. Designed with a smart grid airflow system, they ensure even heat distribution while making sure your delicate food items lift off effortlessly without tearing. Perfectly compatible with commercial and household food dehydrator machines, these sheets are durable, flexible, and completely reusable.

Key Features

  • 100% Food-Grade Silicone: Made from premium, non-toxic, and BPA-free silicone material that is completely safe for direct food contact.

  • Ultimate Non-Stick & Heat Resistant: Highly resistant to temperature. Fruits like mangoes, bananas, tomatoes, and even sticky meat jerky peel off cleanly with zero mess.
  • Reusable & Eco-Friendly: Washable and durable enough to handle hundreds of drying cycles without shrinking or losing its shape.
  • Easy to Clean & Cut: Easily washes under warm soapy water or goes straight into the dishwasher. Can also be cut with scissors to fit any custom tray size or shape perfectly.
