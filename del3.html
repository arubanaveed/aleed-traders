About Us

Who we are

Aleed Traders started as a machinery trading & manufacturing outfit, supplying commercial equipment sourced from China and built in-house. As clients kept asking for websites, branding and marketing alongside their hardware, we grew into a full digital agency too — without ever stepping away from the workshop.
