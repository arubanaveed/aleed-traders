The House

Built on ledgers,
not promises.

Aleed Traders was founded on a simple idea: trade should be predictable. Every shipment we handle is checked, documented and tracked like a line in a ledger — nothing left to chance.

From our base in Lahore, we operate as a bridge between manufacturers, growers and industrial buyers across Pakistan and abroad. Our team manages sourcing, quality inspection, freight and documentation as one continuous chain, so our partners deal with one point of contact from first quote to final delivery.

We work across agro-commodities, textiles, industrial raw material and construction supplies — sectors where a missed deadline or a mismatched grade costs real money. That is why every deal we close is backed by inspection reports, signed contracts and a settlement process both sides can verify.

How We Work

From inquiry
to invoice.

A fixed sequence we follow on every order, regardless of size.

01

Inquiry & Sourcing

You share your specification and volume; we shortlist verified suppliers who can meet both within your timeline.

02

Sample & Quotation

Samples are tested where applicable and a firm quotation is issued, inclusive of freight and clearance costs.

03

Contract & Inspection

Terms are signed, production is scheduled, and an independent inspection confirms quality before loading.

04

Shipping & Clearance

We book freight, manage customs documentation, and track the shipment through to port of entry.

05

Delivery & Settlement

Goods are delivered against signed proof, and the account is settled with a full paper trail on both sides.
